# Introduction

Experiments with 50,000 volts applied to a quartz slide dielectric measuring (measurements).

# Control Experiments
- control.png (1 min of collection): 5 experiments at baseline (no voltage).  The distributions of the calibrated weight measurements are pretty commensurate: all histograms look pretty much the same, as expected.

# Voltage Experiments
voltage.png (~1.5 min of collection):
- experiment 1: voltage applied.  Histograms show weight values gradually increasing.
- experiment 2: voltage applied.  Histograms show weight values gradually increasing.
- experiment 3: voltage still on.  Histograms show weight values localized, as we've peaked (?)
- experiment 4: voltage applied.  Histograms show weight values somewhat increasing, but not as much as experiments 1 and 2.

histograms.png: show all histograms for control.png and voltage.png so we can see everything "at a glance"

# Interpretation
- control.png: all measurements show a consistent calibration when no voltage is applied
- voltage.png: experiments 1 and 2 show a causal change in the distribution when voltage is applied
- voltage.png: experiment 3 seems to suggest that the applied force has "topped out" and remains consistent.  Thus the histogram resembles the control experiments
- voltage.png: experiment 4 should resemble experiments 1 and 2.  Perhaps we did not provide enough "rest" between experiment 3 and 4?
